## Source-Backed Issue

The source supports that subscription cancellation, auto-renewal disclosure, informed consent, and cancellation friction can create enforcement and customer-trust risk.

## Checklist Draft

### 1. Offer Clarity

- Does the signup page clearly explain recurring charges?
- Does it state the billing frequency?
- Does it state when the first charge happens?
- Does it avoid hiding key billing terms below the main action button?

### 2. Auto-Renewal Disclosure

- Is auto-renewal disclosed before purchase?
- Is renewal timing clear?
- Are renewal charges described plainly?
- Are cancellation charges, if any, disclosed before signup?

### 3. Consent Evidence

- Can the business show what the customer agreed to?
- Is the consent action clear and intentional?
- Are checkbox, button, or checkout records preserved?
- Can the exact terms shown at signup be reconstructed?

### 4. Cancellation Path

- Can a customer find cancellation instructions easily?
- Is cancellation available through the same general channel used to subscribe?
- Are unnecessary steps removed?
- Is the customer told when cancellation takes effect?

### 5. Post-Cancellation Confirmation

- Does the user receive confirmation?
- Does the confirmation include cancellation date?
- Does it explain any final charges?
- Does it avoid pushing the user back into another subscription unintentionally?

### 6. Risk Review Notes

- What part of the subscription flow is most confusing?
- Where might a customer feel trapped?
- What proof exists that terms were clear?
- What should be reviewed by a qualified legal/compliance professional?
