# Budget ROI Policy

## Current Status

Real spending is disabled.

## Future Principle

The AI should allocate budget toward the best measured ROI.

## Rollout

1. Simulation-only budget
2. Approval-required microbudget
3. Pre-approved tiny budget
4. ROI-aware reinvestment
5. Larger autonomy only after repeated proof

## ROI Logic

- Unknown ROI: tiny test only
- Negative ROI: stop, revise, or bury
- Low positive ROI: hold and improve
- Strong ROI: repeat cautiously
- Repeated strong ROI: scale slowly within cap

## Scale Rule

Do not jump from tiny tests to large spend.

Use gradual increases only when proof exists.

## Required Proof

Every spend must have:

- linked opportunity item
- spend amount
- expected outcome
- receipt or proof
- measured result
- revenue, if any
- profit or loss
- ROI calculation
- next budget recommendation
